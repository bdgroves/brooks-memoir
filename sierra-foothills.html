The Sierra Foothills

Growing up in Groveland, California — a Gold Rush town in the Sierra Nevada foothills on the road to Yosemite. Hippie Bro and the duct-taped Datsun, Crazy Irvin, Frank from the Dam, the Iron Door Saloon, and long roads east over Tioga Pass to Mono Lake and the desert beyond. Real and imagined, remembered from memory. Start with the map, then wander.

The Territory

Groveland & the Sierra Nevada

A little town in the foothills on Highway 120, the road to Yosemite — Gold Rush country, an hour up from the valley floor. From here the stories run east over Tioga Pass to Mono Lake and the eastern Sierra, and south into the desert toward Death Valley and Trona. Most people can't find Groveland on a map. That was always part of the charm.
