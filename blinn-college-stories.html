The Blinn College Stories

Two years in Brenham, Texas, from the fall of 1988 through the spring of 1990. A kid from the Sierra foothills of California gets sent to the oldest junior college in Texas, in the middle of German farm country, and slowly figures out who he's going to become — one wonderfully strange Saturday at a time. Start at the top, or wander. Written from memory, thirty-five years on.

Where This All Happened

Brenham, Texas

Tucked into Washington County in the triangle between Houston, Austin, and College Station — deep in German-Texas farm country, and about eighteen hundred miles from the Sierra foothills of Groveland, California. Most people can't place it. That was rather the point.

Blinn College Stories · A Story

Across the Hall

A California kid and an East Texas forestry major who smelled like a health-food store lived across the hall in Memorial Hall. Vitamins by the tackle box, PTL tapes under the bed, soil that must never be called dirt, and a first trip to the ballot box in 1988. Nearly forty years later, Todd is still one of the strangest and nicest people I've ever known.
